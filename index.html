A few questions

Four quick ones, so the sessions fit you. You can skip this and change it any time.

What should we call you?

Just for your own screen. Your icon is built from the name — same name, same creature.

Anything you're working around?

Leave a body part alone if it's fine. Go easy means we'll still use it, just rarely. Avoid means we won't program it at all.

Where do you train?

This rules out the movements that need room, or that your downstairs neighbours would hear.

Can you take three steps in any direction?
Can you jump?

How long have you been training?

This only gates complexity — snatches and man makers shouldn't turn up in week one.

Roughly what do you weigh? Optional — only used to estimate calories.
Leave it blank and we'll assume 70 kg and say so.

What have you got?

Bodyweight is always in. Turn on whatever else you can actually reach.
